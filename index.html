About

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Harvard University, on the Bachelor of Science track, who spends as much time writing code as running CAD. My current research at the Harvard Microrobotics Laboratory focuses on tools for ocean exploration and bio-data recovery, and I've spent several summers building software for manufacturing and robotics teams.

I like problems that sit between hardware and software — control interfaces for lab equipment, tracking systems for factory floors, tools that make a physical process legible on a screen. Outside the lab, I'm an ardent double bassist and a musical theater technician.
