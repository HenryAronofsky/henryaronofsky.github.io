About

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Harvard College who spends as much time writing code as running CAD. My current research at the Harvard Microrobotics Laboratory focuses on tools for ocean exploration and bio-data recovery, and I've spent several summers building software for manufacturing and robotics teams.

I like problems that sit between hardware and software — control interfaces for lab equipment, tracking systems for factory floors, tools that make a physical process legible on a screen.
